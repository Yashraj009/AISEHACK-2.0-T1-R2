T5  IS C-BAND BLIND TO RICE, OR DOES IT SIMPLY MISS THE EVENT?
==============================================================================
  Our rice channel is the Capella 6 -> 19 June brightening (+3.23 dB for rice against
  +0.07..+1.12 for everything else), driven by 17.3 mm of rain in the six hours before
  the 19 June overpass (e12, Open-Meteo). Sentinel-1 flew 12 June and 24 June, so it
  STRADDLES that event without sampling it. Testing the closest available pair:

  crop             n   12->24 Jun dB
  Bajra          147         +1.940
  Cotton         449         +1.788
  Groundnut      220         +2.059
  Maize           54         +1.787
  Rice            86         +2.184

  rice +2.184 dB vs rest +1.882 dB, Mann-Whitney p = 0.0699

  Rice brightens MOST, which is the same direction as our Capella channel -- but the
  margin is +0.30 dB against our +3.23 dB, and p = 0.07 is not significant.
  CONCLUSION: 'C-band is blind to rice' is NOT established. What is established is
  that a 12-day C-band VH series does not separate rice here, which is consistent
  with a days-scale flooding event falling between two acquisitions.

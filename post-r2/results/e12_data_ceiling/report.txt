==============================================================================
3.  WHERE WEATHER IS ACTUALLY USEFUL: TIMING, NOT RANKING
==============================================================================
  A spatial ceiling of zero does not make weather useless. Our rice signature is the
  6->19 June brightening (+3.23 dB for rice vs +0.07..+1.12 for everything else),
  and both Coding Bits and Megalodon report 19 June as a rain day (12.5 mm in the
  preceding six hours; 21.9 mm on the day). The MECHANISM of our own crop map
  depends on a weather fact.

  So the question worth asking of a weather API is not 'can it rank farms' (no) but
  'can it pin the rain to the acquisition hour'. Capella overpasses:
    20250606  07:25 UTC
    20250619  02:14 UTC
    20250814  03:11 UTC
    20251013  02:26 UTC

  Open-Meteo's archive gives HOURLY precipitation free and without a key, which is
  what Coding Bits used. OpenWeatherMap's history endpoint is paid and no better
  resolved for this purpose. Testing Open-Meteo now.

  fetched 408 hourly records, 4-20 June 2025
    2025-06-06 07:00 UTC  ->  6 h before    0.0 mm   24 h before    0.1 mm   whole day    0.0 mm
    2025-06-19 02:00 UTC  ->  6 h before   17.3 mm   24 h before   29.0 mm   whole day    7.4 mm

  This is the fact our rice channel rests on, fetched independently rather than
  taken from a competitor's writeup.

==============================================================================
CONCLUSION
==============================================================================
  Anything on a cell larger than the village is arithmetically incapable of ranking
  farms within it. That prices out every weather and reanalysis product for the
  ranking job in one line, and it is measured on our own farms rather than borrowed.
  What remains worth pursuing is 10 m products - and their value has to be shown,
  not assumed, because a ceiling is an upper bound and not a promise.

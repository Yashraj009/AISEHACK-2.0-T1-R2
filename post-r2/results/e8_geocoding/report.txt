==============================================================================
OUR GEOCODING RESIDUAL, BY LEAVE-ONE-OUT OVER THE SUPPLIED GCPs
==============================================================================
  prep_r2.geocode() calls reproject(gcps=...). GDAL with 225 GCPs and no explicit
  order fits an order-3 POLYNOMIAL -- the same class Coding Bits measured at ~8 m
  and replaced with a spline.

  Residual AT the control points would be a rigged comparison: a thin-plate spline
  interpolates every control point exactly, so its error there is zero by
  construction. Both are scored leave-one-out instead, which is what matters for a
  farm boundary sitting BETWEEN control points.

  Our median plot is 0.274 ha, a 52.4 m square.
  (Coding Bits quote 24.7 m for theirs, so our plots are larger and more
   forgiving of the same absolute error.)

  date          n       poly1       poly2       poly3         TPS     median LOO error, metres
  20250606    225        5.62        2.93        3.00        1.41
  20250619    225        7.08        3.39        3.85        1.99
  20250814    225        7.25        3.51        3.88        1.77
  20251013    225        6.60        2.83        3.38        1.80

  date                  poly1       poly2       poly3         TPS     95th percentile, metres
  20250606              16.18       17.69       15.75       12.10
  20250619              20.84       20.75       18.80       15.39
  20250814              21.55       22.75       20.15       15.22
  20251013              18.97       20.61       18.20       15.13

  Our order-3 polynomial: 3.62 m median LOO error.
  Thin-plate spline:      1.78 m.
  Difference:             +1.83 m, against a 52 m plot (3.5% of a plot side).

  Order 2 median: 3.16 m -- BETTER than the order 3 GDAL picks by default.
  With 225 GCPs GDAL selects order 3, and on this geometry that overfits: the
  extra cubic terms cost +0.46 m of held-out accuracy on every date.

  VERDICT, three parts:
   1. The premise does NOT transfer as stated. Coding Bits measured ~8 m for
      their polynomial; ours is 3.6 m median -- under one 5 m base cell, and
      7% of a 52 m plot side. Our fit is roughly twice as good
      as the one they rejected, so their 8 m is not our number.
   2. But the SPLINE still halves it, 3.62 -> 1.78 m median and
      18.5 -> 15.2 m at the 95th percentile. The tail is where this
      bites: a 19 m error is 35% of a plot side, which is
      boundary mixing on the worst-placed farms, and erosion is our only guard.
   3. The cheapest win is not the spline at all: forcing order 2 recovers
      0.46 m for a one-argument change, because GDAL's automatic order-3
      choice is overfitting our GCP lattice.

  So: ADOPT, but not for their reason and not necessarily their method. Order 2
  first because it is nearly free; the spline if the tail matters after that.
  Caveat, stated: this measures the GCP fit only. It cannot see a common-mode
  error affecting every GCP equally -- the class Megalodon's geoid bug belonged
  to, which per-date consistency cannot catch either. Our absolute registration
  against the vendor's geocoded product remains a separate, unmeasured question.
